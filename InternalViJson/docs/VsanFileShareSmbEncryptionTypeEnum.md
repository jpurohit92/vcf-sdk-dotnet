# Vcenter.ViJson.OpenApi.Model.VsanFileShareSmbEncryptionTypeEnum
Enumeration of the SMB file share encryption type.  Possible values: - `disabled`: Disable the encryption for a share - `mandatory`: Traffic to a share must be encrypted.      Clients that do not support encryption will be denied on accessing the share - `FileShareSmbEncryptionType_Unknown`: represent the value when the lower version client cannot recognize the enum value
